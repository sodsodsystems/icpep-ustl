01 / THE KUMUNIDAD

Built together.
Moving forward.

We are the ICpEP.SE UST-Legazpi Chapter—the student community of the Institute of Computer Engineers of the Philippines at the University of Santo Tomas–Legazpi.

Through academic learning, technical practice, leadership, and collaboration, we support students as they grow into responsible, professionally prepared computer engineers.

KumEng is our shared identity: a Filipino twist on “ComEng,” rooted in the warmth and resilience of our Bicolano community. Each generation builds on what came before.

Our mission & vision +

Our mission

The ICpEP.SE–UST-Legazpi Chapter shall promote the advancement of Computer Engineering by providing its student members with academic, technical, professional, and leadership development opportunities. In coordination with the University of Santo Tomas–Legazpi, the College Adviser, and ICpEP.SE Regional 5, the organization shall represent the interests of Computer Engineering students, strengthen student participation, foster cooperation among its members, and uphold integrity, accountability, and professionalism in all its programs and activities.

Our vision

A unified and academically progressive organization of Computer Engineering students of the University of Santo Tomas–Legazpi, recognized for developing competent, responsible, and professionally prepared future computer engineers in collaboration with ICpEP.SE Regional 5.

WHAT WE WORK TOWARD

Space to learn.
Room to lead.

  • Strengthen academic knowledge and technical competence.
  • Develop leadership, teamwork, and professional readiness.
  • Represent students and connect members across year levels.
  • Collaborate with ICpEP.SE Region 5 and the wider engineering community.
  • Uphold integrity, accountability, and responsible representation.
