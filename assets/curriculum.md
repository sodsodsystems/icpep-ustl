# Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering Curriculum Map

**Program:** Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering  
**Curriculum basis:** CMO No. 87, s. 2017, Revised Edition, SY 2022–2023  
**Total curriculum units:** 181

> This program-level copy excludes student grades, name, and identification details.

## Reading Guide

- **Semester groupings:** Subjects are grouped by year level and academic term only.
- **Prerequisites:** The source curriculum map does not list prerequisites. The order and grouping of subjects in this document do not imply prerequisite relationships.
- **Foundation courses:** These courses are conditional and apply only to students who lack the corresponding entrance requirements. They appear separately from the four-year curriculum.
- **Curriculum edition:** Revised Edition, SY 2022–2023.
