## Vision

A unified and academically progressive organization of Computer Engineering students of the University of Santo Tomas–Legazpi, recognized for developing competent, responsible, and professionally prepared future computer engineers in collaboration with ICpEP.SE Regional 5.

---

## Mission

The ICpEP.SE–UST-Legazpi Chapter shall promote the advancement of Computer Engineering by providing its student members with academic, technical, professional, and leadership development opportunities. In coordination with the University of Santo Tomas–Legazpi, the College Adviser, and ICpEP.SE Regional 5, the organization shall represent the interests of Computer Engineering students, strengthen student participation, foster cooperation among its members, and uphold integrity, accountability, and professionalism in all its programs and activities.

---

## Goals

1. **Advance Computer Engineering education** by supporting activities that strengthen the academic knowledge and technical competence of UST-Legazpi Computer Engineering students.
2. **Promote student development** by providing opportunities for leadership, collaboration, professional preparation, and active organizational participation.
3. **Strengthen the Computer Engineering student community** by encouraging unity, representation, communication, and cooperation among students from all year levels.
4. **Establish active coordination with ICpEP.SE Regional 5** through participation in regional programs, professional activities, inter-chapter initiatives, and organizational responsibilities.
5. **Represent UST-Legazpi responsibly** by demonstrating competence, integrity, professionalism, accountability, and respect in institutional and external engagements.

---

## Objectives

The organization shall:

1. Conduct academic seminars, technical workshops, training programs, competitions, and related activities that support the learning needs of Computer Engineering students.
2. Provide students with opportunities to develop relevant technical, communication, leadership, teamwork, and organizational competencies.
3. Encourage members to participate actively in meetings, programs, elections, consultations, and other organizational activities.
4. Represent the concerns and interests of Computer Engineering students through the officers and year-level representatives.
5. Coordinate with the College Adviser and appropriate offices of UST-Legazpi in the implementation of recognized student programs and activities.
6. Participate in programs, conferences, competitions, elections, and other initiatives organized or endorsed by ICpEP.SE Regional 5.
7. Promote collaboration with other ICpEP.SE chapters, academic organizations, professionals, industries, and relevant institutions.
8. Maintain transparent and accountable management of organizational funds, records, membership, and activities.
9. Uphold the Constitution and By-Laws, university policies, and the principles and standards of ICpEP.SE Regional 5.
10. Foster a supportive academic community that encourages members to contribute to the advancement of Computer Engineering within UST-Legazpi and the Bicol Region.

## Theme — KumEng: Refactor

### What is KumEng?

KumEng is more than a nickname — it is the identity of every Computer Engineering student at UST-Legazpi.

Born as a creative twist on "ComEng" (Computer Engineering), KumEng carries a deliberate Filipino character. It is warm, personal, and grounded in the Bicolano identity of the community it represents — a community that is as proud of where it comes from as it is ambitious about where it is going.

The name reflects who we are: not a formal institution, not a distant organization, but a *kumunidad* — a network of students who build together, grow together, and carry each other forward. It speaks in the language of the people it serves, rooted in the culture of Legazpi and the spirit of Bicolano resilience and warmth.

KumEng is also a timeless brand. It was designed not just for one administration or one academic year, but for every generation of Computer Engineering students that will pass through UST-Legazpi. Each batch inherits it, adds to it, and hands it forward — refactored, but never replaced. The name will outlast any theme, any officer, any school year.

As we *create, progress, and evolve*, KumEng remains the constant — the shared identity of a KUMmunity that is always building toward something better.
